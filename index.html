Auxiliary axle for tag trailers

Zero pounds on your tow vehicle’s hitch.

The TrailerToad is a small, USA-built axle that rides between your tow vehicle and your trailer and carries the trailer’s tongue weight itself. You can still back up, and your truck or motorhome stops taking the pounding that can damage your tow vehicle’s suspension, frame and RV structure.
