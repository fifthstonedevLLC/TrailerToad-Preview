How ordering works

Three steps to your Toad

No shopping cart and no freight surprises. Jok prices every order and provides correct freight costs. Toads are delivered via truck freight to commercial and residential addresses.

  1. 1

    Call, text or send the form

    Call, text, or fill out the “Get it right the first time” form. Share your trailer’s weight and what you tow it with, and Jok will tell you whether you need a Toad, a better hitch, or neither.

  2. 2

    Get your freight

    Jok quotes your order with the correct freight cost to your address. Pay the way that suits you: credit card, PayPal, Venmo, bank wire, check or cashier’s check.

  3. 3

    Delivered by truck freight

    We can arrange for shipments to just about anywhere in the USA or Canada.
