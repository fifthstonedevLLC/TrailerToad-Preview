Our story

Why we’ve done this for 20 years

“Reliability and personal service is our brand.”

Trailer Toad · Owner-run

For 20 years, Trailer Toad has made one product nobody else does: an auxiliary axle that takes a tag trailer’s tongue weight off the tow vehicle and carries it on its own steerable torsion axle.

We’ve spent 20 years towing with the Toad ourselves, so the problems it solves are familiar ones: rear suspension working too hard, frame and body flexing, tow vehicle near or exceeding weight limits and a "HARD HIT" at bridges and rough pavement.

That’s also why every order still starts with a conversation. We coordinate each Toad, hitch and freight shipment so it’s right for your rig the first time.
