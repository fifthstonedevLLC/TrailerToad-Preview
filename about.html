Our story

Why we’ve done this for 20 years

For 20 years, Trailer Toad has made one product nobody else does: an auxiliary axle that takes a tag trailer’s tongue weight off the tow vehicle and carries it on its own steerable torsion axle.

Jok has 20 years of firsthand experience towing with the Toad, so the problems it solves are familiar ones: a rear end working too hard, a hitch near its limit, and a jolt at every bridge end.

That’s also why every order still starts with a conversation. Jok coordinates each Toad, hitch and freight shipment so it’s right for your rig the first time.
