Honest advice

You might not need a Toad

Tell us what you tow and what you tow it with. We’ll tell you which of these actually fixes your problem, even if it’s the cheaper one.

Option 1

A Trailer Toad

Takes tongue weight off the tow vehicle entirely. For heavy tag trailers and rigs where the rear end is already working hard.

Best whenYour tongue weight is near or past what your hitch or chassis should carry.
Option 2

A better WD hitch

Sometimes the fix is a properly rated, properly set-up weight distribution hitch.

Best whenYour vehicle can handle the weight but the ride sags, sways or feels loose.
Option 3

A braced receiver

Sometimes the weak point is the receiver itself. Bracing the receiver tube stops it rocking front to rear under load.

Best whenYour hitch is rated under 10,000 lb, or you can feel the receiver move when the trailer is hooked up.
